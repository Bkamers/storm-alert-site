Storm Alert USA

An iPhone app with official National Weather Service alerts for all 50 states explained in plain English, hourly and 7-day forecasts, live radar, hazard guides, an emergency checklist and emergency contacts.

Data comes from the National Weather Service (NOAA). This app is not affiliated with any government agency and does not replace orders from officials. In an emergency, call 911.

Support

Found a problem or have a suggestion? Email brunocamers@hotmail.com. If you can, include your iPhone model, your city and what happened.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026.

Storm Alert USA does not collect, store on servers or share any personal data.

  • Location: if you allow it, the app uses your approximate location only on your device, to ask the National Weather Service for the alerts and forecast for your point. It is not stored or sent to us.
  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • There are no ads, tracking or analytics tools.
  • There are no in-app purchases.
  • Your checklist and selected city are saved only on your device.

To get data, the app connects to NOAA's public services (api.weather.gov and radar.weather.gov), which follow U.S. government policies. TestFlight and the App Store are Apple services, and data they handle follows Apple's privacy policy.

If this changes in a future version, this page will be updated before the change. Privacy questions can also be sent to brunocamers@hotmail.com.
